      Limits

      The defaults come from the Sept 2026 baseline: only 10 of 306 volume-related failures happened between 20 and 65 µL. A well fails if it starts outside that range or would drop below the minimum during the run. It gets a warning if it ends up within the margin of either limit. Use the MSA bias result to set the margin. Your settings are saved in this browser.
